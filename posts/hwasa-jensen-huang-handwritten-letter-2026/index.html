Hwasa Reveals Story Behind Handwritten Letter to NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang: The Unexpected Backstory

Ent. · August 9, 2026 · Published by: World Signal

SEOUL — The unexpected connection between K-pop star Hwasa and Jensen Huang, co-founder and CEO of semiconductor giant NVIDIA, began when the tech mogul publicly praised her music during a South Korean talk show. On the August 9 broadcast of SBS entertainment program My Little Old Boy, Hwasa shared the full backstory—including her initial confusion about who Jensen Huang was and her decision to express her gratitude through a traditional, handwritten letter.

This article details the background of Jensen Huang mentioning Hwasa's songs, Hwasa's initial reaction, her choice of sending a paper letter, and why this unique crossover between global technology and K-pop attracted widespread interest.

The Story Behind Hwasa’s Handwritten Letter to Jensen Huang

Revealed on SBS's Entertainment Show 'My Little Old Boy'

Hwasa appeared on Episode 507 of the SBS entertainment program My Little Old Boy (미운 우리 새끼), which aired on August 9, 2026. The episode featured model Han Hye-jin and Hwasa shopping for bath accessories at Namdaemun Market and enjoying a meal together. During their conversation, Hwasa opened up about the time NVIDIA co-founder and CEO Jensen Huang publicly mentioned her music.

Hwasa revealed that following Huang's mention, she received far more messages and phone calls from acquaintances than when she won first place on TV music ranking shows. At first, however, Hwasa did not even know who Jensen Huang was, so she could not immediately understand why everyone was reaching out. Only after her peers explained that he leads NVIDIA—a world-leading AI hardware titan—did she grasp the magnitude of his praise.

To convey her heartfelt appreciation, Hwasa wrote a personal handwritten letter to Jensen Huang. The choice of an analogue, physical letter to express thanks to the head of a top global technology firm stood out as a memorable gesture. However, details such as how the letter was delivered, its exact wording, or whether Huang sent a reply have not been publicly disclosed.

Which Songs Did Jensen Huang Mention?

'Good Goodbye' and 'So Cute'

During his appearance on tvN's You Quiz on the Block in June 2026, Jensen Huang expressed his fandom for Hwasa, praising her captivating stage presence, vocal range, and dancing skills. He made it clear that he was not merely familiar with her name, but actively followed her musical releases.

The specific tracks Huang named were 'Good Goodbye' and 'So Cute'. 'Good Goodbye' was released in October 2025, while 'So Cute' was a digital single released on April 9, 2026. Because 'So Cute' had come out just two months prior to the interview, Huang’s awareness of her latest single particularly shocked program host You Jae-suk and viewers alike.

Huang commended Hwasa as an extraordinary singer and dynamic performer. The moment highlighted how a prominent international tech CEO engages deeply with K-pop, keeping track of current solo releases beyond general pop trends.

Hwasa’s Initial Reaction and the Significance of a Handwritten Letter

"Thought It Was AI" and Initial Confusion

Hwasa explained her reaction across two separate broadcasts. When she first saw the clip of Jensen Huang mentioning her name and song titles, she admitted she thought the video was an artificial intelligence deepfake. It felt too surreal for a world-renowned technology figure to speak directly about her recent music release.

Over time, as she recognized that Huang was indeed a genuine fan of her music, Hwasa expressed deep gratitude. On My Little Old Boy, she added with candid humor that she didn't even know who Jensen Huang was at first. Realizing the scale of his global influence through the flood of messages from friends, she decided to send a direct personal thank you.

Why the Handwritten Choice Resonated

Rather than making a public social media statement or promotional shoutout, Hwasa chose a handwritten paper letter. A personal letter offers an authentic channel for individual sincerity. However, there is no indication of an ongoing commercial partnership or formal project between Hwasa and NVIDIA; the gesture remains an endearing personal anecdote between a artist and an appreciative listener.

Why Jensen Huang’s Mention Caused Such a Stir

Global Tech Leader's Unfiltered Personal Taste

Co-founding NVIDIA in 1993, Jensen Huang has grown the company into the centerpiece of global GPU architecture and artificial intelligence processing. His casual mention of K-pop preferences on a Korean talk show departed from standard corporate keynotes, creating a widespread buzz that spanned tech investors, music fans, and general audiences alike.

An Unlikely Bridge Between Tech and K-Pop

The story represents a unique moment where the global technology industry and K-pop intersected through personal music taste. Tracks like 'Good Goodbye' and 'So Cute' gained visibility among tech enthusiasts and business professionals who might not typically follow K-pop news, while Hwasa gained symbolic recognition from one of the world's most influential business figures.

Everyday Moments of Han Hye-jin and Hwasa on the Broadcast

Namdaemun Shopping and Culinary Bonding

Beyond the letter discussion, Episode 507 focused on the close friendship between Han Hye-jin and Hwasa. Han Hye-jin planned a relaxing day for Hwasa, who had recently finished a busy world tour schedule. Knowing Hwasa's love for bath routines, the two shopped for bath towels and scrubbers at Namdaemun Market before visiting a favorite local restaurant to enjoy soy-marinated crab rice and braised Deokja (giant pomfret).
