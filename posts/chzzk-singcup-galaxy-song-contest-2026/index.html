Naver’s Chzzk Hosts Streamer Song Competition 'Singcup Galaxy' Ahead of GXG 2026 Finals

Ent. · August 6, 2026 · Published by: World Signal

SEOUL — Naver’s premier live streaming platform Chzzk has officially launched its flagship virtual and live streamer song competition, 'Singcup' (싱드컵), returning this summer with the upgraded 'Galaxy' season. The tournament showcases live vocal performances by Chzzk content creators, with viewers crowning the top stages through an interactive 'Ideal Type World Cup' tournament format. This season has sparked an unprecedented surge of enthusiasm across both human indie broadcast creators and virtual avatar streamers.

Watch Chzzk Singcup Live Broadcasts & Clips

From Solo Streamers to VTubers: A Diverse Festival Roster

Expanded Roster and Newly Introduced Group Category

The defining highlight of the Singcup Galaxy season is the dramatic expansion of its participant pool. In addition to popular indie broadcast creators who built the tournament's legacy in prior editions, virtual streamers (VTubers) armed with elaborate avatar direction, vocal harmonies, and anime-inspired visuals are taking the stage in force. Furthermore, the newly inaugurated Group Category allows creators to form multi-member units, opening the door for duet stages and team vocal performances that were previously unavailable in solo competitions.

Veteran vocalists from past Singcup seasons are also returning to reclaim top positions, creating a compelling competitive dynamic between proven hitmakers and fast-rising rookie creators. With male solo, female solo, and group divisions presenting distinct performance styles, Singcup Galaxy promises a comprehensive music festival experience tailored for live streaming audiences.

Key Event Overview

Singcup Galaxy Quick Facts

  • Event Title: Chzzk Streamer Song Contest 'Singcup Galaxy' (싱드컵 갤럭시)
  • Host Platform: Naver Chzzk (네이버 치지직)
  • Participant Scope: Solo Streamers, VTubers, and Group/Unit Duets
  • Format: Interactive Viewer 'Ideal Type World Cup' Tournament
  • Key Evaluation Window: July 20, 2026 – August 9, 2026
  • Grand Finals Date: September 11, 2026
  • Finals Venue: GXG 2026 Festival Field, Pangyo, Gyeonggi Province

Key Evaluation Period: July 20 to August 9, 2026

How Viewers Directly Influence Tournament Results

Singcup operates as a real-time, participatory tournament where viewer engagement directly determines contestant rankings. Streamers host live music broadcasts and publish highlight clips of their singing stages. The total view duration and 'Likes' accumulated on these official tournament clips dictate which participants advance to the next round.

The crucial preliminary evaluation window runs strictly from July 20, 2026 through August 9, 2026. During this window, fans can actively support their favorite streamers by tuning into live broadcasts, watching competition clips in full, and casting official likes. Platform guidelines emphasize that logged-in users who complete full playback of singing clips provide significantly greater support score than repeated surface-level clicks.

Grand Finals Live at GXG 2026 in Pangyo

Offline Climax at Korea's Leading Urban Game Culture Festival

Contestants who receive top fan support during the preliminary period will earn entry into the grand finals. The final live showdown of Singcup Galaxy is scheduled for Friday, September 11, 2026, hosted on-site at **GXG 2026**, Pangyo’s major urban game culture festival in Gyeonggi Province.

Transitioning from online screen broadcasts to a physical concert stage, the Pangyo finals will bring streamers and VTubers face-to-face with live audiences. The event bridges online digital fandoms with offline game culture festivities, creating a high-energy climax for fans who followed the competition from its July kickoff.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. What is Naver Chzzk's 'Singcup Galaxy'?

Singcup Galaxy is a major streamer song tournament hosted by Naver's live streaming platform Chzzk, featuring solo streamers, VTubers, and group duets competing via live viewer votes and clip engagement.

Q2. When is the key viewer voting and evaluation period?

The preliminary evaluation window runs from July 20 to August 9, 2026. Viewers can support creators by watching singing clips in full and hitting 'Like' while logged into Chzzk.

Q3. Where and when will the Singcup Galaxy grand finals take place?

The finals will be held live on September 11, 2026, at the GXG 2026 Game Culture Festival in Pangyo, South Korea.
