2026 KWDA Lineup: Key Highlights of the 27 Performing Acts

Ent. · August 6, 2026 · Published by: World Signal

GOYANG — The lineup for the 2026 K World Dream Awards (KWDA) features a total of 27 performing acts, ranging from global powerhouses like ATEEZ, RIIZE, and LE SSERAFIM to vocal legends SeeYa, Trot star Park Ji-hyun, and rising rookies Hearts2Hearts. The award ceremony will take place on August 27, 2026, at KINTEX in Goyang, Gyeonggi Province, with Jun Hyun-moo and Jang Do-yeon returning as co-hosts for their fourth consecutive year.

Overview of the 2026 K World Dream Awards

Taking Place on August 27 at Goyang KINTEX

The 2026 K World Dream Awards will be held on Thursday, August 27, 2026, at KINTEX in Goyang, Gyeonggi Province (located at 217-60 Kintex-ro, Ilsanseo-gu, Goyang-si). Selecting KINTEX, a premier large-scale exhibition center in the Seoul Metropolitan Area, allows the event to accommodate both international attendees and massive fandoms. Specific hall entry times and blue carpet operational hours may vary depending on ticket tiers and viewing packages.

The official English name of the ceremony is '2026 K WORLD DREAM AWARDS', abbreviated as KWDA. Originally launched in 2017, the award show celebrates its 10th anniversary in 2026. Designed to go beyond simple chart rankings, the event spotlights the collective contributions of artists, directors, and production crews across the K-pop industry, maintaining its identity as a global K-culture festival combining live performances with awards.

Generational and Genre Breakdown of the Lineup

Co-existence of Global Top Acts and 5th-Gen Rookies

ATEEZ and P1Harmony have cultivated strong international fanbases through extensive world tours, while LE SSERAFIM and RIIZE continue to expand their global streaming and concert footprint. Their inclusion caters directly to both domestic fans and international visitors traveling to Korea. &TEAM's active cross-border presence between Korea and Japan further enhances the festival's international character. JAY B, having built a career across both group and solo activities, bridges different artist generations.

The 2026 KWDA features a notably high proportion of 5th-generation K-pop groups. AHOF, AND2BLE, KickFlip, ALPHA DRIVE ONE, AMPERS&ONE, NOWZ, and IDID form the boy group pillar. Meanwhile, ILLIT, RESCENE, KiiiKiii, MEOVV, and Hearts2Hearts lead the girl group pillar. Presenting these newer acts on a major award show stage offers global fans a comprehensive look at K-pop's ongoing generational transition.

Diverse Genres: Vocals, Live Bands, and Trot

The addition of SeeYa introduces a dedicated vocal group dimension to the idol-heavy lineup. QWER and Hi-Fi un!corn bring live band instrumentation to the stage, while XLOV and RESCENE highlight distinct stylistic directions among newer groups. Even within the same generation, separating performance, vocal harmony, and band arrangements ensures a dynamic, multi-faceted show.

Park Ji-hyun and Seongli introduce solo stages and Trot music elements into the roster. EVAN and JAY B also offer solo performances distinct from group numbers. Consequently, the lineup operates more like an all-encompassing pop music festival than a single-genre concert. Actual performance lengths per artist will vary based on award categories, broadcast timing, and stage production limits.

Background and Significance of the 10th Anniversary

Evolution Since Its 2017 Launch

The K World Dream Awards originally debuted in 2017 as the 'Soribada Best K-Music Awards'. Over time, the ceremony rebranded and updated its operational framework into the present 'K World Dream Awards'. The 2026 edition marks the 10th year of the ceremony. Reflecting this milestone, the event thoughtfully pairs veteran acts with newly debuted generations.

The central theme of the ceremony emphasizes that 'everyone who built today's K-pop is a contributor.' Moving beyond focusing exclusively on winning singers, the event honors production directors, stage crews, and industry staff. It showcases K-pop's growth alongside competitive awards, a vision visually reinforced through the multi-generational, multi-genre performer roster.

Merging K-Pop with Cultural Tourism

The 2026 KWDA aims to boost inbound tourism and enhance the international competitiveness of Korean culture. As a cultural tourism initiative, it encourages overseas fans to visit Korea, attend live award shows and concerts, and utilize local travel, hospitality, and transportation services. Utilizing KINTEX—a venue equipped for international conventions—directly aligns with overseas visitor demand, making the ceremony both prime entertainment and a travel destination.

Global OTT live streaming ensures accessibility for fans unable to attend in person. Online fan voting enables global fans to directly influence award outcomes. Connecting live venue attendance, online voting, and real-time broadcasts expands community engagement well beyond traditional concerts. The 10th anniversary event functions as both a retrospective of past achievements and a look toward future growth centered on rookie acts and global fandoms.

Popularity Award Voting, Live Stream, and Event Info

Popularity Award Decided 100% by Fan Votes

The 2026 K World Dream Awards Popularity Award will be determined 100% by global fan voting. Voting takes place via the fandom platform Fandoor, featuring distinct category voting for group and solo categories. Unlike standard award categories that combine judge scoring and sales metrics, the Popularity Award relies strictly on fan votes. Confirmed performer status and Popularity Award nominations operate under separate guidelines, so fans should check candidates independently.

Live broadcasting will be delivered globally via OTT platforms. Available services and viewing conditions may differ by region, with full platform listings, replay availability, and subtitle options pending final announcement. International viewers are advised to confirm regional broadcasting rights in advance. Schedule times may also differ between the main ceremony, blue carpet, and online streaming feeds.
